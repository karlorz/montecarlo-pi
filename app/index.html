About Monte Carlo Method

The Monte Carlo method estimates π by randomly generating points in a unit square and counting how many fall inside a quarter circle. The ratio of points inside to total points, multiplied by 4, approximates π. This project benchmarks different implementations to compare performance across various computing paradigms.
